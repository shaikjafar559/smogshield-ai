Corsi-Rosenthal Box Complete Blueprint

Required Bill of Materials ($45 total):

  • 1x 20-inch standard box fan (Lasko or similar) (~$20)
  • 4x 20x20x2 (or 20x20x1) MERV 13 pleated furnace filters (~$6-$8 each)
  • 1x Roll of heavy-duty duct tape or gorilla tape (~$4)
  • 1x 20x20-inch cardboard sheet for bottom base
  • 1x Cardboard shroud ring (20x20 inch with 14-inch circular opening)

Step-by-Step Construction Guide:

  1. Cube Formation: Stand 4 filters vertically into a 20" square cube. Crucial: Ensure the directional airflow arrows on all 4 filters point INWARD toward the center of the box.
  2. Bottom Seal: Tape the 20x20 cardboard sheet securely to the bottom. Tape all edges to ensure airtight seal—zero unpurified air must leak from below.
  3. Mount the Fan: Place the 20" box fan on top, blowing UPWARD toward the ceiling. Tape the perimeter fan casing firmly to the top edges of the 4 filters.
  4. The Aerodynamic Shroud: Cut a circular hole (approx 14 inches diameter) into a 20x20 piece of cardboard. Tape this to the FRONT (exhaust) of the fan. This blocks the outer blade corners where backward vortex suction occurs, boosting CADR by 20% and eliminating blade stall.
  5. Placement & Operation: Place in center of room or bedroom 2 feet away from walls. Run on speed 1 (whisper quiet) continuously.
Engineering Note: MERV 13 pleated media captures >85% of particles in the 0.3 to 1.0 micron range (which includes toxic combustion smoke, virus aerosols, and diesel soot). By using 4 filters, total surface area is 1,600 sq inches, making pressure drop 75% lower than single-filter units.
